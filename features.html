The showroom

Logic on one side, FL Studio on the other, spatial on top. What landed in 1.3.2 and 1.3.1, in 1.2.2 and on the Mac, in 1.2, 1.1.2 and 1.1.1, and everything Ovrtone does.

What's new

In the 1.3.1 and 1.3.2 updates
01

Live Loops, played live

Launch quantize from a 1/16 note to 8 bars, and follow actions: after a number of plays a cell stops, repeats, or moves on to the next, a random or a chosen cell.

02

Remote from your phone

Transport, mixer, Live Loops and markers in a browser on the same network, paired with a code or a QR code. Off until you turn it on.

03

Session Players and Chord ID

Detect the chords your MIDI plays, then let a bass or keyboard player follow them in eight styles, on your own instrument plugin.

04

Scores that print

Beams, two voices per staff, chord symbols and articulations. Print or save a PDF, one part per page, or export MusicXML for MuseScore, Sibelius, Dorico or Finale.

05

Alternatives and AAF

Keep named versions of a project in one file, and export the audio tracks as an AAF session for Pro Tools and other DAWs.

06

Tempo and timing

Editable Tempo, Marker and Signature lists, tempo and marker sets, four metronome sounds, 1 to 4 bar count-in, project transpose and SMPTE-locked regions.

07

Outboard gear

I/O Insert sends a track out through your hardware and back, with Ping to measure the round trip for delay compensation.

08

Up to 128 inputs

Record a 48-channel console or a Dante network, every channel to its own track. Input menus come in banks of 16.

09

Flat themes, sharp controls

Flat Night, Flat Slate and Flat Paper, with flat knobs and faders. The 3D controls are rendered at twice the detail for 4K screens.

10

Mixing tools

Solo Safe on reverb returns, Split at Transients for drums, Delete Redundant Points on automation, and Controller Assignments in one window.

11

Files that travel

Bounces are Broadcast Wave with their date and time, and RF64 past 4 GB. Freezes are saved with the project, and audio at another sample rate is converted as it comes in.

12

Tuning and more

Scala tuning for the project, per-note gain and drift in Flex Pitch, Quick Help that follows the pointer, and notes for each track.

13

Play in any key

Musical Typing transposes: press [ and ] or pick a key from the Typing Transpose button, and the C shapes you know play in D, E flat or anywhere else.

14

Browse by keyboard

Arrow keys step through the Browser, playing each sample as you land on it; right and left open and close folders.

What's new

In 1.2.2, and on the Mac
01

Ovrtone for Mac

A beta for macOS 11 and later, one app for Apple silicon and Intel. Your VST3 and CLAP plugins load and open their own windows. Download

02

Four inserts per input

Every Console input now takes four VST3 or CLAP plugins instead of two.

03

Plugins up front

A plugin opened from the Console, or any other Ovrtone window, comes up in front of it. Menus and windows opened after it still go over it.

04

Quicker plugin search

The Search box in the plugin menus takes typing at once, and clicking it keeps the menu open.

05

Clean knobs

No more dark square behind the knobs on light themes.

What's new

In 1.2.0 and 1.2.1
01

The Console

A tracking desk for your interface: up to 16 inputs with trim, polarity, low cut, pan and fader, four cue mixes, talkback, dim, mono and A/B speakers. Record any Console input straight onto a track.

02

Plugins on the desk

Two VST3 or CLAP inserts on every Console input, heard live while you track. Takes record clean.

03

Loudness bounce

Bounce to a LUFS target with a true-peak ceiling, pick a delivery preset, and get a loudness report with the file.

04

Organize Mix Session

Works through the session by itself with a step checklist, then shows its suggestions to review. Gain staging is one tick box.

05

MPE and the Performer

Per-note bend, pressure and slide, plus a Performer that adds breath, vibrato and legato for sax, strings, brass and flute.

06

Articulation Sets

Keyswitch sets per track, chosen in the Inspector and per note in the Piano Roll.

07

Tutorials in 2K

The built-in screen recorder goes up to 2560x1440, and saves your mic and the DAW audio as separate WAV files for clean-up.

08

Drag out

Drag a Browser sample or an audio region out of Ovrtone onto a plugin window or the desktop.

09

Cleaner playback

Renders further ahead for fewer dropouts, and sample-rate conversion through a sinc filter keeps highs clear.

10

Your look

New controls without shadows, a Frosted glass switch in Settings, and the new Flat Grey theme.

11

ChopShop

A sampler and chopper in the installer: slicing, tempo sync, melodic layouts and drum extraction.

12

Undrtone Bass

A bass synth in the installer, with factory banks and an importer for your own patches. The song key follows Undrtone Key.

What's new

In the 1.1.2 update
01

Pro track headers

The name on top and the controls below it, on every track, with Freeze back. Regions get a thin name strip and the lanes alternate.

02

Fit many to tempo

Select several audio regions, right-click, Fit N Regions to Tempo, and type the BPM once.

03

Build-ups in seconds

Trim or cut every selected region at once, and colour several tracks or regions in one go.

What's new

In 1.1.1
01

Playback that holds up

Far less CPU with several plugins (Settings: Process buffer range), no more sound stopping until the audio settings are applied again, and no breakups while the mouse moves over the tracks.

02

Smarter streaming

Short samples play from memory, tempo-following stems are stretched in the background, and an overload fades out and back in instead of clicking.

03

Edit many at once

Drag any selected region to move them all, across tracks. Split at Playhead cuts every selected region, or everything under the playhead.

04

Fine moves

Alt-drag places a region freely, with no snapping. Shift+Alt-drag copies.

05

Mute and Solo sweeps

Press M or S and drag over other tracks to set them all; Alt-click sets every track.

06

Waveforms

Filled and smooth at every zoom, red only where the audio really clips.

07

Precise automation

The value beside the pointer, double-click a point to type one, Ctrl-drag for fine moves, Ctrl+Shift-drag to bend a line. A shows automation on every track.

08

Stereo Out track

Stereo Out pan, and volume and pan automation on its own track, with faders that follow it. Aux tracks can show on the timeline too.

09

One order, EQ box

Ovrtone's effects and your plugins share one order on every strip, Stereo Out included, and the EQ slot shows the curve of an Ovrtone EQ.

10

Project folders

Every project gets its own folder in Documents\Ovrtone, with Audio Files, Bounces, Stems and Beat Store. Bounce in Place lands exactly in time, and closing always asks first.

Full feature list

From first idea to final export

Core

  • VST3 and CLAP hosting with crash isolation: a crashing plugin doesn't take Ovrtone down.
  • WASAPI audio, with optional ASIO.
  • Low Latency Mode, cycle, count-in, varispeed, metronome.
  • 64-bit summing, delay compensation, per-track automation.

Arrangement

  • Tracks, folders, VCAs and aux returns.
  • Audio comping with real crossfaded joins; named and coloured takes.
  • Alt-drag copies; frozen tracks on the Playlist.

MIDI

  • Piano Roll with per-note pitch bend for slide notes, on instruments that take it.
  • FL-style Channel Rack with a shared pattern across clips.
  • Step Editor with velocity and chance lanes.

Mixer

  • Shared plugin and effect order on strips, an EQ slot and plugin presets.
  • Sends, groups, and HUI / Mackie control surfaces.

Spatial

  • Atmos-style object mixing: a 7.1.2 bed plus per-track objects.
  • Binaural render on headphones (SOFA files), speaker render for a monitoring room.
  • ADM BW64 export.

Export

  • WAV, MP3, stems, and a one-shot “For Beat Store” export.
  • AAF import for sessions from other DAWs.
