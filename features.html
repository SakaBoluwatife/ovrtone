The showroom

Logic on one side, FL Studio on the other, spatial on top. What landed in 1.1.1, and everything Ovrtone does.

What's new

In 1.1.1
01

Playback that holds up

Far less CPU with several plugins (Settings: Process buffer range), no more sound stopping until the audio settings are applied again, and no breakups while the mouse moves over the tracks.

02

Smarter streaming

Short samples play from memory, tempo-following stems are stretched in the background, and an overload fades out and back in instead of clicking.

03

Edit many at once

Drag any selected region to move them all, across tracks. Split at Playhead cuts every selected region, or everything under the playhead.

04

Fine moves

Alt-drag places a region freely, with no snapping. Shift+Alt-drag copies.

05

Mute and Solo sweeps

Press M or S and drag over other tracks to set them all; Alt-click sets every track.

06

Waveforms

Filled and smooth at every zoom, red only where the audio really clips.

07

Precise automation

The value beside the pointer, double-click a point to type one, Ctrl-drag for fine moves, Ctrl+Shift-drag to bend a line. A shows automation on every track.

08

Stereo Out track

Stereo Out pan, and volume and pan automation on its own track, with faders that follow it. Aux tracks can show on the timeline too.

09

One order, EQ box

Ovrtone's effects and your plugins share one order on every strip, Stereo Out included, and the EQ slot shows the curve of an Ovrtone EQ.

10

Project folders

Every project gets its own folder in Documents\Ovrtone, with Audio Files, Bounces, Stems and Beat Store. Bounce in Place lands exactly in time, and closing always asks first.

Full feature list

From first idea to final export

Core

  • VST3 and CLAP hosting with crash isolation: a crashing plugin doesn't take Ovrtone down.
  • WASAPI audio, with optional ASIO.
  • Low Latency Mode, cycle, count-in, varispeed, metronome.
  • 64-bit summing, delay compensation, per-track automation.

Arrangement

  • Tracks, folders, VCAs and aux returns.
  • Audio comping with real crossfaded joins; named and coloured takes.
  • Alt-drag copies; frozen tracks on the Playlist.

MIDI

  • Piano Roll with per-note pitch bend for slide notes, on instruments that take it.
  • FL-style Channel Rack with a shared pattern across clips.
  • Step Editor with velocity and chance lanes.

Mixer

  • Shared plugin and effect order on strips, an EQ slot and plugin presets.
  • Sends, groups, and HUI / Mackie control surfaces.

Spatial

  • Atmos-style object mixing: a 7.1.2 bed plus per-track objects.
  • Binaural render on headphones (SOFA files), speaker render for a monitoring room.
  • ADM BW64 export.

Export

  • WAV, MP3, stems, and a one-shot “For Beat Store” export.
  • AAF import for sessions from other DAWs.
